RAMS concepts, explained simply

Companion site of the RAMSysz YouTube channel. Each page below goes with a video and digs into one pillar of RAMS engineering — with definitions, formulas and practical examples.

R

Reliability

The probability that a system performs its required function, without failure, for a given time. MTBF, failure rate, the bathtub curve.

Availability
