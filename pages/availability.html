Availability — the “A” in RAMS

Availability combines reliability and maintainability into one number: what fraction of the time is the system actually ready to do its job?

Definition

Inherent availability: A = MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR) — uptime divided by total time, considering only corrective maintenance.

Key ideas

  • Inherent availability (Ai) — design-level: only MTBF and MTTR, ideal support conditions.
  • Achieved availability (Aa) — adds preventive maintenance downtime.
  • Operational availability (Ao) — the real-world figure: includes logistics and administrative delays (spares, crew, access).
  • Improving A — either fail less often (raise MTBF) or repair faster (lower MTTR): two levers, one target.

Practical example

A train door system has MTBF = 4 000 h and MTTR = 2 h: A = 4000 / (4000 + 2) ≈ 99.95 %. That still means roughly 4.4 hours of downtime per year per door system — multiplied across a fleet, availability targets drive real operating cost.
